About me

I’m a mechanical and aerospace engineering student at Duke, graduating in 2029. I’m interested in aerodynamics, propulsion, and turbomachinery.

What I work on

At Duke Motorsports, I use CFD to change the front-wing geometry and lead carbon-fiber layups. At Duke AERO, I calculate control-surface and fin loads, automate OpenRocket studies, and write Python flight models to compare configurations for a 30,000-foot rocket.

In Duke’s Aeroelasticity Group, I varied plate thickness in Mach 5.8 simulations and analyzed the resulting motion. Outside those projects, I built an app to automate airfoil sweeps and am working through the duct geometry for a compact wind tunnel.

I’m looking for engineering opportunities where I can contribute to design, simulation, and testing.
