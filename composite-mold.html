Composite hat tooling

I modeled a carbon-fiber rice hat and its tooling to work through how the shape could be laid up and removed from a mold. I treated the mold split as part of the CAD work, since the finished shape is only useful if the tooling can release it.

RoleIndependent CAD project
ToolsSolidWorks · STL · Mold design

Parts and molds

I created the hat, head-rest insert, assembly, and positive mold, then divided the tooling into center, corner, side, and split-mold pieces. Separating the tooling gives access to the curved surfaces and allows pieces to be removed in sequence. I exported STL files for fabrication; checking the release sequence and fit of the fabricated tooling would be the next step.

Status

The part and tooling CAD is complete. I have not fabricated the molds or laid up the hat, so the split arrangement still needs a physical check before I can assess release, surface finish, or part quality.
