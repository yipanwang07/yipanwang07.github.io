Compact subsonic wind tunnel

I started this project to give myself a way to test small aerodynamic models. I chose a compact open-return layout and worked on the duct CAD and CFD before committing to a fan or fabrication.

RoleIndependent project
ToolsFusion 360 · SolidWorks · ANSYS Fluent

Work so far

I modeled the contraction, diffuser-side geometry, connectors, and assembly, then used CFD to compare duct shapes. The contraction reduces area to accelerate flow toward the test section. The diffuser expands the flow to recover pressure, but an expansion that is too abrupt can separate and increase losses. I used those competing requirements to guide the duct revisions.

Current status

The next decision is the fan operating point. The fan has to supply enough pressure rise to overcome the tunnel’s losses at the required flow rate; a speed target alone does not establish that it can do so. Mach 0.6 was an early target. I still need the pressure-loss estimate and fan selection before building the tunnel or claiming a test-section speed.
