Automated egg-cooking system

Our EGR 101 client wanted a custardy yolk without spending roughly thirty minutes attending the cooking process. I contributed to the pre-design analysis and technical memo, turning that request into requirements for capacity, timing, materials, cost, and cleaning.

RoleTeam member
ToolsRequirements · Pairwise comparison · Concept selection

Requirements and concept selection

I helped define a budget below $150, capacity for 4–6 eggs, and a weight below 10 pounds. Food-contact parts needed to be food safe and tolerate boiling water. A timer and completion notification addressed the client’s main problem: having to keep watching the process.

I used pairwise comparison in the early concept work to weigh durability, ease of use, dishwasher safety, capacity, and weight. These requirements compete: increasing capacity can increase size and mass, while additional moving parts can make cleaning harder. Ranking them gave the concept comparison a basis in the client’s priorities. This work covered requirements and early selection.
