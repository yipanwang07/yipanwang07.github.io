OpenRocket automation

I wrote a Python/JPype optimizer that runs OpenRocket directly. It screens thrust curves, compares booster–sustainer motor pairs, adjusts the geometry, and filters trajectories against the study’s stability and rail-speed limits. The saved August run contains 2,946 simulations. I added checkpoints and independent worker processes so I could stop and resume a long search without losing completed cases. OpenRocket automation and summer results.

Flight models in Python

I built RocketPy scripts to make the mass, motor, drag, and launch inputs explicit and repeatable. The staged model separates booster flight, sustainer coast, and sustainer burn, including a state handoff when the reference center of mass changes. I compare those runs with OpenRocket under matched inputs before interpreting differences. RocketPy modeling and staging.

Airbrakes and canards

I ran airbrake cases across speeds and altitudes to provide drag data for deployment decisions. The same deployment produces a different load as dynamic pressure changes. For the canards, I calculated lift coefficients and center-of-pressure locations across angles of attack; the controller needs the force and its lever arm to calculate the available moment.

Geometry and loads

I compared nosecone profiles over flight speeds and worked through more than ten canard and fin iterations. More aft-fin area can increase the restoring moment, but it also adds drag, mass, and root bending load. I used fin-loading CFD to connect those geometry choices to reinforcement and manufacturing decisions.

Mesh work

I refined the mesh near the body and control surfaces to represent the steep velocity and pressure gradients. The reported y+ decreased from about 300 to 5, with a minimum orthogonal quality of 0.13 in the revised mesh. I assess those values alongside the wall treatment and force convergence.

Keeping the models usable

I organized the GitHub repository around the RocketPy baseline, model maintenance, Monte Carlo analysis, and OpenRocket automation. I documented the inputs and next steps so a study could be reproduced before adding more variables. The current automation and Monte Carlo guides describe future team work; the completed summer optimizer and staged-model studies shown here are separate earlier projects.
